# 10 — Cost Management

## Objective
Demonstrate cloud cost awareness and budget control.

## Tasks
- Create a budget appropriate to the remaining free-credit balance.
- Configure an alert threshold.
- Review cost analysis.
- Identify the resources most likely to generate cost.
- Deallocate/delete unused resources.

## Evidence
- [ ] Budget
- [ ] Cost analysis
- [ ] Alert configuration
- [ ] Cleanup evidence

## Interview questions
- Why can a stopped VM still incur some costs?
- What are Azure reservations?
- How do tags help with chargeback/showback?
