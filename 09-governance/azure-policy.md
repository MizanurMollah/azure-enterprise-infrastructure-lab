# 09 — Azure Policy

## Objective
Use policy to enforce governance.

## Tasks
- Review built-in policy definitions.
- Assign a low-risk lab policy appropriate for your subscription.
- Example: require a tag on resources.
- Test the policy against a resource.
- Document compliance result.

## Evidence

- [ ] Assignment
- [ ] Compliance result
